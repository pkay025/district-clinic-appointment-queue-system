    thresholds to ...
  - Kelvin reviewed NFR-04, NFR-05 and the Act 843 mapping and
    changed ...

  - Checked the context map renders and corrected ...
  - Confirmed the ISO 25010 characteristic names against the
    2023 standard

Signed: ________________________  (Product Owner / Team Lead)

The team then reviewed it section by section, with each section assigned to the member whose role covers it, and revised it before submission. The thresholds in the NFRs and the WSJF scores were proposed by the AI tool and re-decided by the team.
